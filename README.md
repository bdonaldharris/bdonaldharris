## ⚙️ Currently Building

### [BitVoices Network](https://bitvoices.network) — *Builder-First Platform for Black Tech*

A community-powered platform combining:
- **Builder Profiles** — showcase projects, products, and technical identities
- **Hubs** — discipline and interest-based communities
- **Media & Podcast Layer** — amplifying Black technologist stories
- **Product Storytelling** — structured narratives around what we're building and why
- **Community Events** — connecting founders, engineers, and creatives

> *The goal: a permanent, founder-led digital home for Black builders.*

## 🧱 Builder Philosophy

> *"Build systems that last. Build communities that compound."*

With 20+ years in software engineering, I've learned that the most important things we build aren't just the applications — they're the ecosystems, the narratives, and the networks that make other people's breakthroughs possible.

My work is rooted in a belief that **Black technologists deserve the same infrastructure — platforms, capital, community, and visibility — that every other builder takes for granted.** I'm building that infrastructure, one layer at a time.

Code is the medium. Community is the mission.
